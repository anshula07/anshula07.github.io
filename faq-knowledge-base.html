2. Frequently Asked Questions

Use the FAQs below to quickly find answers to common questions about Sentinel Privilege Manager.

What is Sentinel Privilege Manager?

Sentinel Privilege Manager (SPM) is an endpoint privilege management solution that helps organizations control, manage, and monitor elevated access to applications and endpoints.

Who can request application privileges?

Users can request application privileges when the application requires elevated permissions and the organization's policy allows privilege requests.

Depending on the configured workflow, the request may require approval from an authorized approver.

How long does an approved privilege remain active?

The duration depends on the privilege policy configured by the administrator.

Policies may allow temporary access for a defined period or provide access according to an organization's approved privilege model.

Can an administrator revoke an approved privilege?

Yes. Administrators can revoke access when required by organizational policy, security requirements, or operational circumstances.

Where can I view my privilege requests?

Users can view submitted privilege requests from the My Requests section of the SPM console.

The request status indicates whether the request is pending, approved, rejected, or expired.

Why was my privilege request rejected?

A request may be rejected because it does not meet the configured approval rules, application policy, access requirements, or business justification criteria.

If available, review the rejection reason shown in the request details.

Can privilege access be scheduled?

Yes. If scheduled privilege access is enabled, users can request access for a defined future period according to the applicable policy.

Where can administrators review privilege activity?

Administrators can use the privilege activity and reporting capabilities available in the SPM administrative console to review access events and user activity.

3. Access & Account

How do I access the SPM console?

Open the SPM console URL provided by your organization's administrator and sign in using your assigned credentials or configured authentication method.

What should I do if I cannot sign in?

Verify that your credentials are correct and that your account is active. If the issue persists, contact your administrator or support team.

Note: Do not share passwords, authentication codes, or other credentials when requesting support.

4. Privilege Management

How do I request application privilege?

Sign in to the SPM console.
Open the Privilege Requests section.
Select the application for which elevated access is required.
Enter the required business justification and access duration.
Submit the request for processing.

What happens after I submit a request?

The request is evaluated against the configured privilege and approval policies. Depending on the workflow, the request may be automatically approved or routed to an authorized approver.

How can I check request status?

Status Description
Pending The request is waiting for processing or approval.
Approved The requested privilege has been granted.
Rejected The request was not approved.
Expired The approved privilege duration has ended.

5. Policies & Configuration

What is a privilege policy?

A privilege policy defines the conditions under which users can receive elevated access to applications or resources.

Who can configure privilege policies?

Privilege policies are managed by authorized administrators according to the organization's access-control model.

What should I do if a policy change is not reflected?

Allow sufficient time for policy synchronization and verify that the endpoint is communicating with the SPM platform. If the change remains unavailable, follow the applicable troubleshooting procedure or contact the administrator.

Best Practice: Document significant policy changes and maintain an appropriate review and approval process for administrative configuration.
